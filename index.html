About the Shop

A neighborhood spot, run by Jim, since '94.

Trader J's has been a fixture on Village Parkway for three decades. Jim built the shop on straightforward pricing, honest grading, and a genuinely friendly hello — the kind of place where kids picking their first Pokémon pack feel just as welcome as long-time collectors chasing a key issue.

Come browse a clean, well-organized floor: pull sheets are up to date, the $1 boxes get restocked, and there's no pressure to buy. Just cards, comics, and conversation.
